ABOUT GANITA VENTURES

Direct Trading & Strategic Procurement in Bihar

Headquartered in Puraniganj, Munger, Bihar (811201), Ganita Ventures was founded by Sushil Kumar (Proprietor) with a focus on delivering high-grade chemical supplies, minerals, and institutional consumables. We bridge the gap between heavy manufacturers and local institutions, eliminating middlemen and providing genuine factory pricing.

From supplying bulk Hydrated Lime Powder (Calcium Hydroxide) for municipal water treatment to distributing Stable Bleaching Powder (33% Available Chlorine) for public sanitation and providing turnkey Office Stationery Kits, we guarantee certified quality, full GST compliance, and punctual logistical execution.
